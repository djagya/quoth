# Rye sourdough

My everyday dark loaf, one 1 kg tin.

## Dough

- 350 g whole rye flour and 150 g bread flour (70% rye).
- 400 g water (80% hydration), 10 g salt.
- 100 g active starter (Grisha), 2 tbsp molasses, 1 tbsp caraway seeds.

Mix with a spatula, it is a paste, not a dough. No kneading.

## Proof and bake

- Proof in the greased tin for about 3 hours, until cracks show on top.
- Bake at 250 °C for 10 minutes, then 210 °C for 50 minutes, 60 minutes in all.
- Rest it wrapped in a towel for a full 24 hours before cutting, or the crumb is gummy.
