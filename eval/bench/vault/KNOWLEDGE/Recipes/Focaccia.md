# Focaccia

Ilse's recipe, the one she brought to every party at university.

- 500 g bread flour, 375 g water (75% hydration), 10 g salt, 4 g instant yeast.
- 40 g olive oil in the dough and plenty more in the tray.

Mix, four sets of stretch and folds over the first hour, then overnight in the fridge.
Next day spread in the oiled tray, rest 2 hours at room temperature, dimple with oily
fingers, top with rosemary and flaky salt. Bake at 230 °C for 22 minutes.

Variations: cherry tomatoes and olives in summer; red onion and potato in winter.
