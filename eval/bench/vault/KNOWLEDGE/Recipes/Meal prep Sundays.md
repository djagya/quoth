# Meal prep Sundays

The routine since January 2026, to stop ordering food on weekdays.

- Cook two bases on Sunday afternoon: one legume (dal or chickpea curry), one grain.
- Six glass containers, labelled with the date. Eat within four days, freeze the rest.
- Jasmine rice in the rice cooker at 1:1.2 rice to water; basmati 1:1.5.
- Roast a tray of vegetables at 220 °C for 30 minutes.
- Shopping list goes to the Saturday market at Lindenauer Markt.

Since starting I spend about 40 € a month less on delivery.
