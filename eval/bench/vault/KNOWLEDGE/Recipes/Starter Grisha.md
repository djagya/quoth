# Starter Grisha

My rye sourdough starter, alive since March 2024. Named after the cat a friend had in
Kazan.

- Feeding: 1:4:4 (starter, whole rye flour, water), e.g. 10 g + 40 g + 40 g.
- Lives in the fridge; fed once a week on Sunday, and the day before baking.
- Ready when it has doubled and smells like yoghurt, about 8 hours at 24 °C.
- After a holiday: discard all but a spoon and do two feeds at 1:1:1 in a warm spot.
- Dried backup flakes are in a jar in the cupboard (made in June 2025).
- Grisha does not like wheat flour: once fed with it, it went sluggish for a week.
