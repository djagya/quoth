# Chickpea spinach curry

25 minutes, works with pantry stuff.

- Onion, garlic, ginger, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin.
- One tin of chopped tomatoes and two tins of chickpeas.
- Simmer 15 minutes, stir in 200 g spinach at the very end until it wilts.
- No cashew cream when Jonah eats with us (he is allergic); use yoghurt instead.

Good with naan. Leftovers taste better the next day.
