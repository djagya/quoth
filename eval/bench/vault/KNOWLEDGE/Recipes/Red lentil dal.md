# Red lentil dal

Twenty minutes, one pot.

- 200 g red lentils, rinsed, 800 ml water, 1 tsp turmeric. Simmer 15 minutes.
- Tarka: 2 tbsp ghee, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp black mustard seeds, wait until they pop,
  then garlic, ginger and one green chilli.
- Pour the tarka over the lentils, squeeze of lime, coriander.
- Coconut milk optional, half a tin, makes it milder.

Freezes well in portions. Serve with rice or the flatbreads from the Turkish bakery.
