# The Dispossessed — notes

Read in January 2026. The best book of the year so far.

- Two worlds: Anarres, the anarchist moon with no property, and Urras, rich and unequal.
- The wall at the spaceport opens the book: which side is inside depends on where you stand.
- Shevek's physics: simultaneity, the general temporal theory, which he finally gives away
  to everyone so no state can own it.
- Favourite idea: on Anarres even language avoids "my"; a child says "the" handkerchief.
- Lend my copy to Jonah; then read The Left Hand of Darkness.
