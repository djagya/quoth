# Lisbon, May 2026

9 to 15 May 2026, alone, around the DataTide conference where I gave my talk on
idempotent billing retries (slides in WORK).

## Where I stayed

A friend of Rui's rented me his flat in Alfama, top floor, no lift, view of the river.
70 € a night, cash at the end.

## Food

- Pastéis de nata at Belém, still warm, queue moved fast.
- Bacalhau à Brás at a tiny place in Graça called O Fado Lento.
- Ginjinha in a chocolate cup at Rossio.

## Things I did

- Tram 28 was packed every time; walked most of the hills instead.
- Day trip to Sintra by train from Rossio (40 min). Pena Palace tickets booked online for
  the 9:30 slot, which beat the crowds. Got a proper sunburn on the Moorish Castle walls.
- Met Rui, my colleague from the Lisbon office, for dinner in Cais do Sodré.

Total spend for the week, flights included: about 640 €. The conference ticket was paid by
work.
