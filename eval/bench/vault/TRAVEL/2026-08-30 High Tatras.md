# High Tatras, August 2026

Three days in the Slovak High Tatras with Jonah, 22 to 24 August 2026.

## Days

1. Train to Štrbské Pleso, walk to Popradské pleso, night at a hostel in Starý Smokovec.
2. Rysy summit from the Slovak side, about 5 hours up. The chains near the top are easy in
   the dry. Tea and a slice of cake at Chata pod Rysmi, the highest hut, where everyone
   carries a 10-kilo stone up for good luck.
3. Téryho chata via the Small Cold Valley; cable car down from Skalnaté pleso because
   Jonah's knee hurt.

## Practical

- Trails above the huts close from November to mid-June.
- Bears: we did not see one, but the hut keeper told us to make noise in the forest.
- Jonah paid the hostel, I paid the train tickets; we are even.
- Cheaper than the Dolomites: about 45 € a night with dinner.
