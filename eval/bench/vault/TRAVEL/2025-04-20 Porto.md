# Porto, April 2025

Five days in Porto with Ilse, 14 to 19 April 2025. Flew from Leipzig via Frankfurt; from
the airport the metro (purple line E) goes straight to Trindade, about 35 minutes.

## Where we stayed

Casa do Bonfim, a small guesthouse in the Bonfim district, east of the centre. Quiet street,
a tiled breakfast room, the owner Dona Amélia baked orange cake every morning. 92 € a night
for the double room.

## Food

- Francesinha at Tasca Marujo near Bolhão market: the best of the trip, split one between two.
- Bifana with mustard at the stand by São Bento station.
- Grilled sardines in Matosinhos on the last evening.

## Things we did

- Port lodge tour in Vila Nova de Gaia. I bought a 10-year-old tawny for Mum (she liked it).
- Skipped the queue at Livraria Lello; walked along the Ribeira instead.
- Day trip to Aveiro by train, 1 h 10 min from São Bento, ovos moles and the striped
  houses of Costa Nova.
- Lost my sunglasses on tram 1 to Foz.

It rained on three of the five days. Next time bring a proper rain jacket, not the thin one.
