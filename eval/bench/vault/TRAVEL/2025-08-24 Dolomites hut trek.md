# Dolomites hut trek, August 2025

Four days on the Alta Via 1, 17 to 20 August 2025, solo.

## Route

1. Lago di Braies to Rifugio Biella. Easy start, crowded lake.
2. Biella to Rifugio Fanes. Blister on the left heel by noon; taped it.
3. Fanes to Rifugio Lagazuoi. A storm came in after lunch, so I skipped the Kaiserjäger
   via ferrata and took the normal path.
4. Lagazuoi down to Passo Falzarego, bus to Cortina.

## Practical

- Huts booked in January; by March most were full.
- Half board was about 75 € a night; cash only at Biella.
- Water: refill at every hut, carry 1.5 litres.
- Next time: break in the boots before the trip, and bring sock liners.
