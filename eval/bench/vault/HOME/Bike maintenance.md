# Bike maintenance

My steel touring bike from 2019, the daily commuter.

- Chain: clean and lube every 300 km, or after every rain ride. New chain every
  3,000 km.
- Tyres: Schwalbe Marathon 40 mm, 4 bar front, 4.5 bar rear.
- Brake pads replaced in April 2026 at the annual service at Radwerk Süd, 95 € in all.
- Lights: dynamo hub, so nothing to charge.
- Lock: the folding lock, plus the frame lock at the office.
- Next service: April 2027.
