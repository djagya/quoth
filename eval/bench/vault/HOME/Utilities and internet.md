# Utilities and internet

- Electricity: green tariff with the city utility, 52 € a month in advance payments.
  Meter reading in the first week of December; photo it and upload it in their app.
- Internet: 250 Mbit fibre, 39.99 € a month, contract until March 2027; cancel or switch
  in January 2027.
- Router admin password is on the sticker under the router; Wi-Fi name "Kantine".
- Broadcasting fee is paid quarterly from the main account.
- Water and heating are in the warm rent (see the lease).
