# Decision: no cargo bike for now

Looked at a long-tail cargo bike with electric assist, about 3,800 €.

Decided against it, for now:

- No safe place to keep it: the building has no bike room, and it does not fit in the
  stairwell.
- The city's free cargo-bike rental (book a day in advance) covers the few big trips.
- Revisit if we move to a flat with a courtyard.
