# Ilse and Mark's wedding

Pécs, 20 June 2026, in the garden of her parents' house.

- Dress code: garden formal. I wore the light grey suit, no tie.
- Stayed at Hotel Makár, two nights; booked through her cousin.
- My toast in Hungarian: "Egészségetekre!" (to your health). Practised it for two weeks;
  her grandmother cried.
- Gift: a framed print of the 1890 map of Pécs, found through an antiquarian in Budapest.
- Dinner went on until 2 am; the band played csárdás and then 80s pop.
