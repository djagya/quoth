# Tidepool — v1 plan

The original plan, November 2025.

- Wi-Fi straight to the router, deep sleep for 5 minutes between readings.
- InfluxDB 1.8 on the Raspberry Pi for storage, Grafana on top.
- Enclosure: 3D-printed in PETG.
- Sensors: just the BME280.
- Budget: 60 €.
