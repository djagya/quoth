# Knee physio

Right knee, pain under the kneecap since January 2026, worst on stairs and downhill.

- Physio: Lene Brandt at the practice on Gohliser Straße, six sessions prescribed in
  February 2026, paid by insurance.
- Diagnosis: patellofemoral pain, weak hips; nothing structural.
- Exercises: Spanish squats 5 × 45 s, slow step-downs from a 20 cm step, glute bridges,
  side planks. Every other day.
- Avoid steep downhill running for now; walk the descents.
- Ice does little; the exercises help. By May the stairs were fine.
