# Running plan 2026

Goal: the Muldental half marathon on 11 October 2026 in under 1:55.

- Four runs a week: Tuesday intervals, Wednesday easy, Friday easy, Sunday long run.
- Intervals: 6 × 800 m at 4:40/km with 400 m jog.
- Easy pace around 6:10/km, heart rate under 150.
- Long run on Sunday builds to 18 km by mid-September, then taper two weeks.
- Strength twice a week for the knee (see the physio notes).
- Shoes: Saucony Ride for everything, a second pair from March.

Changed in May: Wednesday became a rest day after the knee flared up; three runs plus
one bike ride.
